## Decisions made with the user

- **Enemy health:** the item part is measured, and the vitality investment bonus turned out to
  be most of it. The user's full-build screenshots are the calibration fixture.
- **M6 scope:** templates (M7) and the skill-path planner (M8) are moved out. The coach uses
  templates first, with a model later.
- **Roles:**
  - Mo & Krill: Tank + Support.
  - Lash, Vindicta and Violet: stay Off-carry.
  - Deadman Danny: Off-carry.
- **Coach wording:** approved as shown in PR #119. The anti-synergy reasons were then rewritten
  in plain English.

## Caught on the way

- **End-of-game net worth barely separates roles (0.86–1.11×).** Net worth at mid-game, from
  the performance curve at 50% of 35–45-minute matches, does. Its `game_time` is a
  percentage, not seconds.
- **One failing coach message could silence the coach for the session.** Fixed: the coach
  falls through to the next candidate.
- **Upgrades:** the planner replaces the component, so "first spirit item" fired on an upgrade
  and a false power spike was possible. Fixed: the context puts the component back.
- **The first panel wiring re-derived the tip on every render.** The slider could change it, and
  a seen tip could fall out of memory. Fixed: each answer is frozen with its action.
- **Windows:** stopping a background `next start` left the server running, and one browser
  check hit the old build. Fixed by killing the port's owner (saved to memory).
